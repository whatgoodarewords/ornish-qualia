Four small paper refinements.

Optional proposals from Opus 5.5, shown at the same scale. The main demo already has your requested compact fields, aligned headings and message selection. These four proposals are not applied or approved.

01

One continuous paper tail

Benefit: the corner feels part of the slip; tradeoff: a drop-shadow filter may add rendering cost per bubble, which has not been measured.

Look at the lower outside corner of each bubble.

02

Let each sender read as a group

Benefit: names belong more clearly to their messages; tradeoff: each change of sender gets 6px more separation.

The proposed label-to-bubble gap also tightens from 5px to 3px.

03

A tighter, aligned Memory list

Hover over a saved item, or

Benefit: the list aligns and its hover feels pressed into paper; tradeoff: that well extends 10px into the sheet's side padding.

04

A Memory glyph with a state

Click either bookmark to compare both states.

Benefit: Memory matches the other 24px glyphs and signals its open state; tradeoff: a filled bookmark adds a little visual weight.
