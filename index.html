PAQPre-emptively Answered Questions

The following words were written by a human

What is this about?

Effing the ineffable. A space to live new culture! It’s somewhere between a research symposium, the best house party you’ve ever been to, and a curated festival in private nature. Something like this hasn’t happened before, so honestly, we’re not quite sure.

This is more for doing phenomenological research than talking about research, but there will assuredly be talks.

This is part of a greater phenomenological research month, continuing for the three weeks following the event.

So it’s a party. What does that mean?

It means it’s fun. Dancing. Immersive, playful, curious, reverent, irreverent, in on the joke. We believe fun leads to focus, and focus leads to insight.

Can anyone come?

No. It’s curated for character, via application.

I want to bring a dependent.

Children, pets… all sentient beings are welcome to apply. We do not discriminate. Everyone in attendance must apply and will be held to the same standard of curation.

Is this a night thing?

All chronotypes are welcome. There will be day and night programming. We’ll start more day-focused and end more evening-focused. The goal is for everyone to leave feeling better in their bodies than when they arrived.

Will this be overstimulating?

Everything is opt-in. Parts may be, for some. This is an advanced event. The land is vast, and soft, with many cozy nooks and quiet zones. We’ll have an outdoor sound curfew most nights. There will be an arc.

Can I bring something to life here?

Yes. It’s a hybrid: curated from the top down, co-created from the bottom up.

Can we create a guild?

Yes! Guilds are like camps, but you don’t need to worry about food, water, or shade. Paradise has you covered.

What about food?

Four meals a day: Second Breakfast at 9am, Lunch at 1pm, Dinner at 7pm, and Breakfast at midnight :)

Will there be a potluck?

Yes, there will be a phenomenological potluck the likes of which the world has never seen.

I’m sober. Will that be a problem?

No, quality is a hell of a drug. You’ll fit right in.

Any weird rules?

No alcohol, no ketamine, no smoking, no small talk.

How long is the event?

Five or six nights.

Where do I sleep?

Camping, glamping, vans, and private cabins.

When can I arrive?

Wednesday or Friday. Arriving after 7pm on Wednesday costs €100 more. We want you here for the whole experience. If you’re a weekend warrior, you can afford it.

Is this part of something bigger?

The party kicks off a month of phenomenological research. Afterwards, there’s an optional three-week residency: more time to follow a question, bring something to life, and live some life! Come for the party, or stay for the month.

Is this event for profit?

It will most assuredly lose money.
